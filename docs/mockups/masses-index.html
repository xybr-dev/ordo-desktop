A. Masses → Upcoming, interactive

What Ordo opens on (Decisions 64, 250). Each Celebration is a group with its date, season, liturgical colour, Mass count and a readiness roll-up; its Mass plans sit under it with time, language, choir, presider and readiness (Decision 83). Anticipated Masses sort with the Celebration they belong to (Decision 85). A row opens that plan in the Mass Planner. The amber button at the top right is the backup reminder (Decision 198).

B. Filtered

Language, choir and readiness filters narrow the plans; a Celebration with no matching plans says so instead of disappearing.

C. A Celebration's … menu

Group actions: add a sibling Mass, reuse the plans for another Celebration, export the Celebration Package (Decision 191), or archive.

D. New Celebration

Creation is Celebration-first (Decision 84): the Celebration's details and how its first Mass plan starts. Next: add the Mass opens Add a Mass for the new Celebration; Do this later leaves it with no plans.

E. Add a Mass

From Add Mass on a Celebration (Decision 84). Date, time, language, choir, presider and location, then the template. The Celebration's day is the default; the evening before asks to confirm Anticipated (Decision 85). Presider and location start as the parish defaults; the template recommendation follows the language (Decision 79).

F. An anticipated Mass with a visiting presider

The evening before, confirmed as Anticipated. A name typed in Presider that isn't a regular presider stays on this Mass only.

G. No template in the language

Tagalog has no template, so the plan would start empty with a readiness warning rather than borrow another language's (Decision 79).

H. Duplicate a Mass into another language

Duplicate within the Celebration (Decisions 74, 81). Choosing another language shows what carries over and what is left to finish.

I. Reuse for another Celebration

From a past Celebration's menu, or Reuse on one of its plans (Decision 36). Choose the plans and their new times, then an upcoming Celebration or a new one. Readings come over marked Needs review; a season change is flagged too.

J. Reuse into a new Celebration

One plan, reused into a Celebration created on the spot. Advent differs from the source's Ordinary Time, so the seasonal items are called out.

K. History

Celebrations whose date has passed move here by themselves; that isn't archiving and doesn't claim the Mass was presented (Decision 37). Older groups start collapsed.

L. Archive

Archived plans are hidden from Upcoming and History. Deleting is only possible here.

M. Templates

The slot lists new Mass plans start from (Decision 76).

N. First start: Set up Ordo

A new installation opens here, with no rail and no Skip (Decision 218).

O. Starting a new parish

Only the parish name is required. The first regular presider becomes the default for new Mass plans.

P. A new parish with nothing planned

After setup, Masses offers the three ways to begin (Decision 218).
