A. Responses & Prayers, interactive

The Library's Responses & Prayers view (Decision 257). Each entry is a list of exchanges: an optional prompt that stays on the operator's screen, then the congregation's response, which is projected. Edits save as you type, and the slides underneath update.

B. Filtered by role

Filter by language and liturgical role. Entries in different languages are separate entries and are never kept in step.

C. Header override

The response header normally comes from the Mass plan (TUBAG: in Cebuano plans). An entry can hide it or override it; each Mass item can still change it again (Decision 84).

D. Plans on an earlier revision

Every change saves a new revision. Plans that link the entry keep the revision they use and show Update available until someone reviews it; this line says how many already do.

E. New entry

New creates an entry in the filtered language and role, puts the cursor on its name, and starts one empty exchange. It shows as Incomplete until the response is written.

F. Archived entry

Archived entries are inspect-only; restoring one makes it editable and linkable again.
