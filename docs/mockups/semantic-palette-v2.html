A. Colour: Preparation (warm paper) and Presenter (graphite)

Every swatch reads its value live from ordo.css, which carries DESIGN.md's frontmatter. When the tokens are revisited, this sheet and every regenerated screen follow without edits. The left panel follows the page's Light/Dark switch, as Preparation does; the right is always graphite, as Presenter is.

Presenter

B. Operational states: colour, icon or shape, and words together

No state is colour alone. Live, Freeze, Blackout, warning, unavailable and information each pair a word with a stable marker shape. Selection is a tinted fill; keyboard focus is the separate two-layer blue outline; they can coexist.

C. Liturgical colours are content, not chrome

Purple, red, green, rose and white describe a Celebration or a congregation Theme. They never mark selection, state or focus, and never recolour the operator shell.
