A. Lyrics: write each section once

Daygon Ta, Koro San Roque. The left pane lists the reusable sections with their slide counts and how often the Sequence sings them. Each section's words are written once, here the Koro, which the Sequence sings twice. Reordering sections in this list changes only the writing order.

B. Sequence: the sung order

The same three panes. Available sections sit on the left with an explicit Add. The centre lists section occurrences by position (02 Koro, 04 Koro), never renamed copies. The selected occurrence offers Repeat, move and remove, and expanding one shows its generated slides in order. Try Repeat, the arrows, Alt+↑, or Delete on a focused row.

C. Removing an occurrence

Removing an occurrence needs no confirmation: the section and its lyrics stay in Lyrics. The next occurrence takes the selection, and Undo puts it back where it was.

D. Empty Sequence

Sections exist but nothing is placed yet. The Arrangement is Incomplete and gives a Mass no slides until the first occurrence is added; the Sequence tab carries the marker.

E. A section not yet in the Sequence

Adding a section in Lyrics never places it. A quiet Add to Sequence beside it inserts it after the selected occurrence, as a visible step. Unused sections are allowed without a warning.
