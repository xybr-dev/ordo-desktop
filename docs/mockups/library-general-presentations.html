A. General Presentations, interactive

The Library's General Presentations view (Decision 257): announcements, thanks and appeals made of text or image Slides. Edits save as you type.

B. Text and image Slides

An image Slide holds one picture, fitted inside the safe area and never cropped, with a description that isn't projected. A Slide can override the default auto-play time (Decision 258).

C. New entry

New starts one empty text Slide and puts the cursor on the name. It shows as Incomplete until a Slide has text.

D. Archived entry

Archived entries are inspect-only; Mass plans that use one keep it.
