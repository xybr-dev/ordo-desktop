A. Live on the Gloria, interactive

Click the Presenter, then press Insert (or Insert after … above the timeline). Program freezes on the Gloria. Press S, type pan, use ↓ and Enter. The new Song lands after the Gloria with its first slide in Preview; Program stays frozen until Space. While the dialog is open, no single-key Presenter command runs.

B. The Insert chooser

Anchored at the top of the window, with the destination spelled out: after the current Mass item, in its section. S, R and T pick a kind; Program is already frozen (Decisions 12, 258).

C. Quick Insert, before typing

Centred and keyboard-first. With nothing typed it lists the choir’s repertoire; the active result is previewed on the right as canonical slides.

D. Typing filters

Matches titles first, then other names and first lines, ignoring accents. The reason shows when it isn’t the title.

E. No Song matches

The way out is to type it for this Mass. Enter opens the editor with the title filled in.

F. A Song typed for this Mass

A blank line starts a new slide. It is Mass-local: saved in this plan for crash recovery and offered to the Library only through a review after Mass.

G. Inserted: Preview on the new Song, Program still frozen

The item is marked Inserted live in the timeline and the Ordo rail. Program still holds the Gloria; Space takes the Song title live.

H. Text or image

A slide typed now, or a picture from this computer, previewed in the canonical frame before it is inserted.

I. A Response sequence

Chosen from Responses & Prayers with ↑ ↓ and copied into this Mass; the Library entry doesn’t change.
