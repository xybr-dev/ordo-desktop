A. Live on the Gloria, interactive

Click the Presenter, then press Insert (or Insert after … above the timeline). Program freezes on the Gloria and the Insert panel opens on Song. Type pan, use ↓ and Enter. The new Song lands after the Gloria with its first slide in Preview; Program stays frozen until Space. While the panel is open, no single-key Presenter command runs; Alt+R and Alt+T switch to Response sequence or Text or image.

B. The Insert panel

One panel over the Cue timeline for every kind; Program, Preview, Freeze and Blackout stay in view beside it (Decision 290). It opens on Song with the search focused: the choir’s repertoire first, then Other Songs in the Library, all reachable with ↓.

C. Typing filters

Matches titles first, then other names and first lines, ignoring accents. Repertoire matches stay on top; Halad comes from the rest of the Library because its first line matches.

D. A Song outside the choir’s repertoire

Any Song in the Library can be inserted. The row says it isn’t in this choir’s repertoire; inserting doesn’t add it there.

E. No Song matches

The way out is to type it for this Mass. Enter opens the form with the title filled in.

F. A Song typed for this Mass

In the same panel, with Back to search. A blank line starts a new slide. It is Mass-local and offered to the Library only through a review after Mass.

G. Inserted: Preview on the new Song, Program still frozen

The item is marked Inserted live in the timeline and the Ordo rail. Program still holds the Gloria; Space takes the Song title live.

H. A Response sequence

The same search, list and preview as Songs, over Responses & Prayers. Copied into this Mass; the Library entry doesn’t change.

I. Text or image

A slide typed now, or a picture from this computer, previewed in the canonical frame.
