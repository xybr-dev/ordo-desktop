A. Arrangement History

History in the Arrangement header opens a slide-over over the editor, which stays as it was underneath. Editing sessions are grouped, with when, what and how many revisions; creation, import and restore stand on their own. Expand a session to reach its individual revisions.

B. Compare a revision

Selecting a revision opens a nested comparison while History recedes. Overview, Lyrics, Sequence and Slides compare the current Arrangement with that revision, marking what differs. Nothing changes until you restore.

C. Restore, second stage

Restore this revision turns the whole footer into the confirmation: what's affected on one row, Keep current and Restore entire Arrangement on the next. Restoring replaces the whole Arrangement, never single lines.

D. After restoring

The restore becomes a new revision and every earlier one stays. The toast offers Undo, which is itself another revision.

E. Song History

A Song's History covers only its own identity and metadata; each Arrangement keeps a separate History.
