A. Masses → Templates, interactive

Templates are preparation content under Masses (Decision 77). Each row shows the language, how many slots it has, how many plans it made, and what it's recommended for; the star marks the language fallback (Decision 79). Edit opens the Template Editor; try changing an item, a rule or a section.

B. A template's … menu

Duplicate, make it the language's fallback (one per language), or Archive. Archiving only stops New Celebration offering it.

C. No templates yet

The same list with nothing in it (Decision 220). Plans can still be made without a template.

D. New template

Only a name and a language. The name must be unique.

E. A new template

Opens with the six Mass sections and nothing in them.

F. Template Editor

The planner's three panes (Decision 77): the outline by Mass section, the selected item, and the template's details. This Song slot keeps its place and moment; each plan picks the Song (Decision 76). The banner says existing plans keep their copy (Decision 78).

G. A conditional item

A rule includes or omits the item from Celebration metadata, with the result for sample Celebrations underneath (Decision 75). A Celebration without the season the rule needs stays unresolved.

H. Check against a Celebration

Pick a sample Celebration to see what the rules would omit for it. Nothing is saved.

I. A Response sequence

Concrete content: copied into each new plan and edited there for that Mass only (Decision 112).

J. A Reading slot

Holds the role; each plan's readings fill it.

K. Section actions

Rename, add above or below, move, remove. A section with items can't be removed, nor can the last one (Decision 177).

L. Adding an item

Adds to the selected section, after the selected item.
