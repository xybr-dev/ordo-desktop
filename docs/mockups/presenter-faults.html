A. Live on the Gloria, interactive

Use the buttons to cause a fault, then drive the Presenter with the keys (click it first). Every fault fails closed: nothing is claimed to be on screen that may not be, and only Space or the primary control puts content back.

B. Display 2 disconnected

Program says DISCONNECTED instead of claiming the Gloria is still visible. Output never moves onto the operator screen. The session continues: the operator has moved Preview to the next slide; Freeze and Blackout don’t apply.

C. A different display was connected

Ordo doesn’t use it. Space is refused with the reason; the way forward is to reconnect Display 2 or choose a display deliberately.

D. Display 2 is back

Its window is recreated black. Nothing shows until the operator presses Space or Resume Output, which takes Preview live in one step (Decision 272).

E. A later slide fails to render

Found while live on the Gloria: the First Reading title can’t be drawn. Its card becomes Output unavailable with the reason; it stays in the timeline, and the urgent strip says what Ordo will do.

F. Reaching the failed slide while live

Program goes black (LIVE · black · Cue unavailable), never showing the failure; the next valid slide goes live as usual. Space on it is refused.

G. Reopened after Ordo closed unexpectedly

No dialog: Ordo reopens straight into Presenter. Congregation output is black; Preview, the inserted Song and the operator’s place are restored. What Program showed before is private context only. Space takes Preview live and dismisses the strip; Exit Presenter stays available.

H. Reopened, but Display 2 isn’t recognized

Recovery enters Disconnected instead of guessing a display.
