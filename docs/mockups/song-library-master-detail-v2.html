A. Songs, interactive

The Library's Songs view at 1366 × 768. Search and filters stay on the left; the selected Song's identity and Arrangements fill the right, and selecting another result changes only the right pane. Try searching an alias such as Kyrie or a lyric such as pan ug bino, filter by Choir, or press New song or Edit details.

B. Filtered by Choir

Filters apply to single Arrangements: a Song stays only when one of its Arrangements meets every filter, and the count says how many match out of how many it has. In the detail, matching Arrangements come first and the others stay visible but subdued.

C. Search by lyric

Search covers titles, aliases, composers, Choirs, languages and full lyrics. A result that matched on something other than its title says why, here with the matching line.

D. New song with possible duplicates

Only the primary title is required; nothing is saved until Create song. Possible existing Songs sit behind one collapsed disclosure, expanded here. Selecting one opens a preview while the form recedes, and Back returns to the form exactly as typed.

E. After Create song

The new Song is selected with a valid No Arrangements yet state; adding one is the next action, not a requirement.

F. No songs match

An empty result names the search and filters and offers the one correction. It never shows first-use guidance.

G. Empty Library

The same workspace with nothing in it: Create song first, Import .ordo second, and three short steps in the detail pane. No onboarding page replaces the real interface.

H. Archived Song

With Show archived on, an archived Song can be inspected but not edited. Its active Arrangements say they're available once the Song is restored.

I. Edit Song details

Edit details in the Song header opens the New song surface with the Song's current title, composer, aliases and usual moments. Changes are staged until Save details, which stays disabled until something changes. The notice says where the title and credits also appear before anything is saved (Decision 276).

J. 1920 × 1080

The same composition with a wider browser pane; nothing else changes.
