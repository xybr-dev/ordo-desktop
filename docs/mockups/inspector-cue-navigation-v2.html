A. An operator-only trigger

The Inspector steps through the selected item's Cues in order, counting operator-only triggers: Cue 1 of 4. A priest's line projects nothing, so its congregation frame is black and the line, role and timing stay outside the frame as private context. Selecting a Cue in the editor moves the Inspector, and the arrows move the editor's selection.

B. The item boundary

At the last Cue, Next is disabled and the Inspector says where to go: navigation never slides into the next Mass item. Changing items stays an outline action.
