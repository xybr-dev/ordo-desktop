A. Present with incomplete content

Present finished autosave, prepared a candidate from the current revision and ran preflight. Two Offertory Cues can't produce a frame, so one centred, viewport-bounded modal opens over the dimmed planner. Review and fix is recommended and focused; it opens Readiness at the first finding. Present available content is the acknowledgment itself, so there is no checkbox. Cancel, Close or Esc return to the planner unchanged and create no snapshot.

B. After "Present available content"

Presenter opens in the same window with congregation output disabled; clicking Present never creates an output window. Passed checks collapse into one line, and what still needs attention stays above the timeline with its corrective action. The unavailable Cues stay visible and inspectable in the timeline but can never be taken live. With clean or warning-only content there is no modal at all: Presenter opens straight into this state.
