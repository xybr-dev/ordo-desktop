A. Visual Slide Grid

Projectable Cues are canonical thumbnails, never narrower than 300px, with captions outside the frame. Operator-only triggers, Waits and Holds break the grid as full-width compact rows, so the priest's line sits exactly where it falls in the order. Unavailable Cues stay in place with the operator-only Output unavailable placeholder. Groups collapse by their header; the active Cue keeps the focus outline.

B. Compact Cue List

The same Cues as one dense list: number, a small canonical thumbnail or the operator-only marker, the first line, and the Cue type. The choice of Grid or List is the operator's and persists; no window size changes it.

C. A narrower window keeps the mode

A 1100×768 window: the grid drops to two columns rather than shrink frames below 300px, Ordo becomes its tab, and Grid stays Grid.
