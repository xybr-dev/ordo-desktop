A. The whole flow, interactive

One working planner. Everything below is also reachable here, and each change can be undone from its toast. A suggested path:

  1. With 5. Gloria selected, press + in the Mass outline. The menu says where the item goes; change it if you like, then choose Song.
  2. The new item is created and selected, and the Song picker opens. Search, open a Song, pick an Arrangement, then press Use … arrangement.
  3. Press 1 incomplete · 1 warning. The Offertory finding names the problem; its action takes you to the item's Choose another Arrangement.
  4. In the picker, the scanned score is marked unavailable; choose the text Arrangement instead. Readiness drops to one warning.
  5. Open the Entrance Song, press Customize for this Mass, select a Koro and edit it: both Koro occurrences change, unless you use Edit this occurrence only.
  6. Press Theme: Ordo Black, try the Themes on the preview, and review the Kyrie's override before applying.

B. Add Mass item

An anchored menu under +. It states the destination, after the selected item in its own section, and lets the operator change it before choosing. Opening or dismissing it changes nothing; choosing a type creates, selects and autosaves the item at once, with Undo in the toast.

C. Choosing the Song for a new item

Choosing Song creates an incomplete item and opens the staged picker over the planner. Selecting an Arrangement only stages it: the preview and sequence update, and the item changes only when the sticky Use … arrangement is pressed. › or Esc closes without changing anything.

D. Replacing an unavailable Arrangement

From the Offertory's notice, the picker opens on the same Song with its current Arrangement marked: it can't be chosen again because its image asset is missing. The text Arrangement is staged as the replacement.

E. Readiness

The header button states the counts in words. Its slide-over sits over the Inspector, leaving the outline and editor usable. Findings group into Incomplete presentation and Review recommended in Mass order; a finding's title selects its item and keeps the panel open, and its action closes the panel and focuses the control that fixes it.

F. Mass Theme

A full-window slide-over that previews each Theme on the selected Cue. The choice is staged until Apply. Items that keep their own Theme are named up front and never silently replaced.

G. Theme Overrides

Review opens a child slide-over; the chooser recedes but stays in view. Each overridden item compares its current override with what it would inherit. Use Mass Theme stages removal, reversible until the parent applies everything as one change.

H. Customize for this Mass

The deliberate step from library content to a Mass-local copy. The banner says the changes belong only to this Mass. The sequence can be reordered and trimmed, and repeated sections stay linked: editing one Koro edits both unless Edit this occurrence only forks it. Here a Bersikulo has been removed and the Koro reworded.

I. Return to library arrangement

From the banner, one focused confirmation lists the local changes being dropped. Undo brings them back.

J. Replacing a customized Song

Committing a different Arrangement for an item with local changes opens a nested review over the picker. Ordo never merges local edits into another Arrangement; Back returns to the unchanged picker selection.
