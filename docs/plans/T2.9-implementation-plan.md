## Outcome

Ordinary library evolution becomes recoverable, entity by entity, without any Song-merge machinery:

- The Arrangement editor header shows **Arrangement details**, **History** and a **More Arrangement actions** overflow; the Song detail header shows **History** and a **More Song actions** overflow. Each **History** opens a full-app floating right slideover over the untouched editor or Song detail, after flushing pending autosave.
- History lists that entity's revisions newest first: grouped editing sessions (**Edited lyrics · 8 changes**, when, how, revision count) that expand to individual revisions, and standalone creation / import / restore events. A Song's History never lists Arrangement revisions and an Arrangement's never lists Song revisions. A revision that only approved a section's pagination reads **Approved pagination**.
- Selecting a revision opens a nested, read-only **Compare revision** slideover while History recedes. Arrangements compare through **Overview**, **Lyrics**, **Sequence** and **Slides** (canonical frames); Songs through their identity and metadata. Changed lines and occurrences are marked; unchanged ones stay visible.
- **Restore this revision** turns the comparison footer in place into a warning-tinted, uncarded confirmation: the impact on its own row, **Keep current** and **Restore entire Arrangement** (or **Song**) on the next. Restoring commits one new `restoration` revision, rebuilds the editor on the restored content, and shows **Restored the revision from 10:42. Every earlier revision is still in History.** with **Undo**, which is one more restoration.
- **Archive song** / **Archive arrangement** (overflow) confirm in a compact dialog, then archive without touching any other entity's archive state, offer **Undo** in the toast, and move selection: the next visible active Song, otherwise the previous, otherwise focus on the active-list empty state; an archived Arrangement returns the operator to its Song.
- Archived Songs and Arrangements are inspect-only with an **Archived.** banner and **Restore**, stay reachable through **Include archived**, and keep their History viewable. Their overflow offers **Restore** and **Delete permanently…**.
- **Delete permanently…** opens one reference-safety dialog. Blocked: it groups and names every blocker (child Arrangements, Mass plans, Presentation snapshots, packages, other records) and offers no deletion. Safe: it states what is erased, including the owned revisions, and deletes with no Undo.
- Nothing anywhere offers merge, survivor, redirect or reversal.

## Shaping constraints

- **The domain decides; storage enforces atomically.** Verdicts, blocker grouping, selection after removal, change labels, the comparison and the legacy-restore state rule are pure domain functions. Storage re-checks deletion preconditions inside the deleting transaction (a reference can appear between review and commit) and applies the restore rule against rows it reads in the restoring transaction; it decides nothing the domain did not define.
- **No Phase 3 scope.** No table can hold a durable reference and T2.9 must not invent one. `song_references.rs` is the one place Phase 3 adds Mass-plan and snapshot queries; until then the E2E's reference is a test fixture (Decision 2).
- **Whole entity, one revision.** Restore commits one `restoration` revision through the existing `restore_revision`; its Undo is another. No partial restore control exists anywhere (Decision 143).
- **Legacy payloads never claim a change or an approval they did not record.** Format 1/2 revisions have no section states: they never show a state difference, and restoring one never invents an approval (Decisions 5, 6).
- **One editor session at a time.** A restore or an archive replaces what the editor shows by rebuilding the editor (new `EditSession`, new History session), never by patching under a live session (Decision 8). History opens only after a successful flush (Decision 142).
- **Inspect-only means no writes.** Archived entities get no edit session (already true), no Restore this revision, and an overflow with only Restore and Delete permanently.
- **No work on the GPUI thread.** Storage reads and writes go through the store's worker; History rows and change labels are built with `cx.background_spawn`; the Slides comparison composes through the existing off-thread frame path and cache.
- **Copy.** Verbatim from the approved mockups above; where none exists, from the decisions' own wording, listed for approval (Decision 15). No task ids in visible copy. Refusals are mapped to operator copy, never shown raw (T2.7a rule).
- **Editor ground rules.** Real parish store rig in tests, waits for **Saved**, real keys and clicks, exact strings and ids; F6 order actions, primary list, main editor, Inspector, with slideovers trapping focus; list rows `Role::ListItem` with names and selected/current state, icon buttons named with tooltips; every refusal surfaced; the UI never constructs `PaginationState::Approved`; content reloads only on `EditSessionEvent::ContentReplaced` (a restore rebuilds the editor instead).
- **Changes paths.** `crates/ordo/src/history/`, `crates/domain/src/song_library/lifecycle.rs`, `reference_safety.rs` and `crates/storage/src/song_references.rs` are new as written. `tests/e2e/songs/history-lifecycle.*` does not exist: the E2E lives in `crates/ordo/src/songs/e2e.rs`. Files outside the line (`revision_changes.rs`, `songs/lifecycle/`, ui primitives, store, storage revisions, test files) are added by WP10's amendment.

## Decisions made while planning (owner to confirm)

1. **The two Changes-line domain files hold the pure rules and types; `SongLibrary`'s methods stay in `interface.rs` and delegate.** `reference_safety.rs` takes `DurableReference`, `DeletionBlocker`, `DeletionVerdict`, the verdict builder and new blocker grouping; `lifecycle.rs` takes the lifecycle action sets and selection after archive. Re-exports from `song_library` are unchanged, so no caller moves. *Reason:* the fields are private to `interface`. *Alternative:* `interface/lifecycle.rs` child modules with the archive and delete methods moved (more churn, same behaviour).
2. **Durable references come only from `storage::song_references`; the E2E's reference is a planted fixture.** `durable_references(tx, target, planted)` is the one lookup every deletion review and delete uses. Phase 2 has no table for it to query, so it returns the planted references, held in the worker's memory and written only by `#[cfg(feature = "fault-injection")]` `Storage::plant_reference` / `remove_reference`. A planted reference is an ordinary `DurableReference::MassPlan(EntityId)`, so the dialog shows exactly what a real one will. Phase 3 adds query arms for Mass plans, snapshots and packages in this file. *Reason:* no stored record can name a Song or Arrangement today; a reference table would import Phase 3 schema. *Alternative:* migration 0008 with a generic `durable_reference(referrer_kind, referrer_id, song_id, arrangement_id)` table (durable across reopen, but a second source of truth beside the real Mass-plan rows later).
3. **Permanent deletion is one immediate transaction in storage that re-checks and never cascades.** `delete_arrangement`: refuse unless archived in its own right and `durable_references` is empty; delete `section_occurrence`, `arrangement_section`, `arrangement_language`, `arrangement_choir`, `arrangement_occasion`, then every `edit_revision` of the Arrangement in one statement (self-references resolve at statement end), then the row. `delete_song`: refuse unless archived, owning no Arrangement (archived ones included) and unreferenced; delete aliases, moments, occasions, its revisions, the row. A schema test proves every table with a foreign key to `song` or `arrangement` is cleared here or blocks. No tombstone: after the next reload the deleted Arrangement's section and occurrence ids are no longer reserved, which is safe because nothing durable may name them (that is the deletion precondition). *Alternative:* a reserved-id tombstone table (a migration against a v4-collision risk).
4. **Archive and restore stay outside History.** They change visibility, not content: no revision (as today), no History row. ## Risks / unknowns

- **Deleting revisions vs FK checks.** `edit_revision` references itself through `parent_id` and `restored_from_id`. One `DELETE … WHERE arrangement_id = ?` resolves at statement end; a row-by-row delete fails. The schema test and the fault-injection loop must prove the order before the UI depends on it.
- **A reference appearing between review and commit.** Only the storage re-check prevents stranding it; the dialog must treat `DeletionBlocked` from the commit as "blocked" and redraw, not as a generic failure.
- **Reserved ids after deletion.** Decision 3 drops them on reload. If a later snapshot keeps section ids without a reference the lookup can see, deletion could strand them; Phase 3/4 must add the snapshot arm to `song_references.rs` before any snapshot can name a section.
- **Fixture references are not durable.** They live in the worker's memory and vanish on reopen; the E2E plants after its last reopen that needs them. A reload-with-blocker test needs Decision 2's alternative.
- **Editor rebuild on restore.** Rebuilding drops expansion and selection; the restore lands on the same tab with selection reset. Tested; if it reads as jarring, Decision 8's alternative applies.
- **Change classification noise.** T2.7/T2.8 sessions mix structural revisions that may classify as `Several`; the label falls back cleanly. The first open of a long History decodes every document: measure a 500-revision fixture and page the list if it exceeds the interaction budget.
- **Two-stage footer focus.** The overlay's footer arrow-walk assumes three fixed handles; the two-stage footer needs its own without breaking the containment tests.
- **Icon-only Menu.** `Menu` is a qualified Select variant; the icon trigger must keep the Menu role, the name and the popup's Escape handling (`escape_with_the_menu_open_closes_only_the_menu`, `presenter/mod.rs:5853`).
- **Copy approval.** Decision 15 lists strings with no mockup; T2.9c's PR body lists every one again, and review may change them.
- **Mockup conflict on merge.** Decision 13 departs from an approved mockup; if the owner reads AC9 as "no enabled action", WP8 adds the disabled row and WP9's view test narrows to enabled items.
- **Local time.** `current_local_offset` can fail; the model takes the offset as input, tests pin it, production falls back to UTC as `recovery.rs:213` does.
