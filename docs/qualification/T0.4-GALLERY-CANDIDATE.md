# T0.4 Canonical Slide Layout Gallery — approved

Status: **approved** at the T0.4 manual gate (script C in

## What this covers

Every adopted template, label/background variant, and representative overflow
case, produced by the real Rust renderer through the same `1920x1080` logical
composition the operator viewports, Program, congregation output, snapshots,
and emergency export consume:

- Templates: Lyrics, Response, Reading identification, Reading passage, Psalm,
  Song identification, Celebration title, General Text, General Image, Black.
- Label variants: section label present vs `label-off` (header absent);
  Response/Psalm authored headers present vs absent vs empty (absent collapses
  the zone, empty reserves it — distinct compositions, pinned by
  `rendering --test golden`).
- Background variants: theme background vs required black slide
  (`black.png` vs every other slide; distinct `background` intents).
- Overflow representatives: `lyrics-overflow-warning` (geometric overflow plus
  lyric ceilings, complete frame beside three warnings) and
  `celebration-title-overflow` (four rendered lines vs the three-line ceiling).

## Approved type levels

`DESIGN.md` owns these numbers; this summary records what the gallery
approved. The projected levels are **display `90px` /
passage `70px` / meta `50px`** — each exactly +25% from the PR 5 provisional
`72px` / `56px` / `40px`. Line advance stays `ceil(size * 1.15)`: 104 / 81 /
58. The Celebration/Reading-identification exception is **title
`135px`/weight `800` (50% above display, advance `156px`) with a
Celebration-only optional Normal-Case subtitle at `68px`/weight `300` (75%
of display, advance `79px`)**: both titles stay exactly as authored (mixed
case, never uppercased), both hug the safe-area left edge, the Celebration
subtitle sits below the title, title plus subtitle center together above the
unchanged bottom-left date, Reading identification centers vertically above
its unchanged bottom-left reference, and absent/empty/whitespace subtitle
reserves nothing. **ReadingPassage-only `88px`/weight `400`**
(`70 * 1.25 = 87.5`, rounded; advance `102px`) applies only to
`TemplateKind::ReadingPassage`, left-aligned and top-anchored above the
unchanged `50px` bottom-left reference; GeneralText stays at `70px`.
**Psalm and Response bodies are content-sized**: each takes the largest rung
that fits from Psalm `140/120/105/90px` at weight `700` or Response
`160/140/120/100px` at weight `800`, centered. **Song titles** lead at
`100px`/weight `700` in authored case. The shared `90px` display size stays
for Lyrics. Operator, UI, and lab-control typography are unchanged except the
`113px`/weight `800` projected-response token. The long cases were judged
comfortably readable at these levels.

Consequences of the +25% step, reviewed in the re-blessed goldens
(`cargo xtask bless-golden-frames` diff): header/body rects shift by the new
measured heights (e.g. meta header `46px` → `58px`, display content `83px` →
`104px` per line), glyph advances scale ~25% with identical glyph ids and
fallback routing, and the cross-platform invariance probe now composes 4 body
lines where it composed 3 (same 3 authored lines; content `416px` vs `862px`
available, still `fit=fits`). `lyrics-cebuano-one-line` carries
`Dalaygon Ka, Ginoo sa gugma;` so the corpus keeps a true one-line Cebuano
case at the `90px` size (the longer line now wraps to two).

## How this was generated

From the worktree root, through the running canonical-slide lab's own
pipeline (not a direct renderer call): every fixture loads with the lab's
loader, composes on the production canonical worker behind
`CanonicalCommand::Compose` at the default Theme and 100% density,
rasterizes at 100% with the lab's solid-gray GeneralImage stand-in, and
finishes through `ordo::canonical_views::finish_frame` — the same handoff
the operator viewports consume:

```text
ORDO_GALLERY_CAPTURE=$PWD/design-references/ordo-canonical-slide-layouts \
  cargo test -p ordo --example canonical-slide-lab --locked gallery_capture
```

The capture test
(`crates/ordo/examples/canonical-slide-lab.rs::gallery_capture_through_the_lab_pipeline`)
writes `design-references/ordo-canonical-slide-layouts/<name>.png` (all `1920x1080`
finished pre-chrome pixels) plus `capture-rows.md`: per-fixture provenance
carrying the visible frame, the wrapper, and the diagnostics riding beside
each capture. The table below is that manifest's exact output. Regenerating
reproduces these bytes; a recapture that changes them needs a fresh
approval.

To review live instead of (or as well as) these captures:

```text
cargo run -p ordo --example canonical-slide-lab
```

`F2` cycles every fixture below, `F3` the viewport preset, `F4` the Interface
scale, `F5` the target density; diagnostics ride beside the scaled frame.

## Long Cebuano/English readability

Measured at the approved levels, from the real renderer (see table):

- `reading-passage-cebuano` and `reading-passage-english` (the long running
  passages, ReadingPassage-only `88px`): each wraps to 2 rendered lines, `fit=fits`, no warnings.
  The short English fixture wording matches the Catholic USCCB text at
  https://bible.usccb.org/bible/john/1; no additional Scripture text is added.
  `general-text-mixed` stays at `70px`.
- `lyrics-mixed-four-line` (long mixed Cebuano/English/Tagalog, 90px): 4
  rendered lines, `fit=fits`; the only warning is the expected two-phrase
  target note (4 phrases), not a geometric overflow.
- `lyrics-tagalog-three-line`: fits with the expected phrase-target note
  only; no geometric overflow. `psalm-cebuano` and `psalm-header-empty`
  (1 authored line, 4 rendered lines on the Psalm ladder) fit
  with no warnings and no geometric overflow. The two Psalm specimens carry only the
  short refrain for `Salmo 103:1-2, 3-4, 9-10, 11-12` as sourced from
  https://sulugoon.com/readings/2026/09/13/; no additional Psalm text is added.
- `response-cebuano` (`TUBAG:` + authored-case body on the Response ladder
  at weight `800`): 1 authored line, 2 rendered lines, no warnings.
- `reading-identification` (Sirac title preserved sentence case at 135px/800):
  1 authored line, 2 rendered lines, `fit=fits`, no warnings.
- `reading-identification-roma` and `reading-identification-mateo` (Roma/Mateo
  titles preserved sentence case at 135px/800): each 1 authored line, 3
  rendered lines, `fit=fits`, no warnings.
- `celebration-title` (title preserved mixed case at 135px/800 + subtitle
  `National Catechetical Day` at 68px/300): 1 authored body line, 4 rendered
  lines (3 title + 1 subtitle), `fit=fits`, no warnings; the three-line
  ceiling counts title lines only.
- Intentional overflow fixtures still warn beside complete frames (see
  `lyrics-overflow-warning`, `celebration-title-overflow` rows:
  `celebration-title-overflow` carries 4 title lines vs the three-line
  ceiling).

The reviewer judged every case, including the long ones, comfortably
readable; the result is recorded in `T0.4-MANUAL-GATE.md` script C.
