# Bundled asset sources

- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Noto Sans are from `google/fonts` commit `ade3d1533e06b2b1462ffcde8e08b129627ca360`.
- Lucide SVGs and its combined ISC/MIT notice are from `lucide-icons/lucide` commit `796dad298f8d78c5da204c3e62a5ed93c2bfcd1e`.
- `icons/filled/` holds Ordo-derived filled forms of those Lucide outlines for the current rail destination (Decision 300): the outer shape is filled and its details are cut out through an SVG mask, and Library and Activity use a 2.75px stroke instead. They are covered by the same Lucide notice.
